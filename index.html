Getting Started

Onboarding & Authentication

Experience seamless, flexible entry into Innervy. Sign in securely using Google OAuth 2.0 or jump straight into Guest Mode with local database isolation.

🔒
Privacy Guaranteed: Each user account receives an isolated Hive database stored locally on your device. Switching accounts or using Guest Mode automatically switches database isolates.
🔑

Google Sign-In

Authenticates via official Google OAuth 2.0 protocols. Displays user profile details (display name, email, and avatar) across the dashboard.

Tap "Sign in with Google"
👤

Guest Mode

Instant, single-tap access without account creation. Perfect for users who prioritize total anonymity and offline usage.

Tap "Continue as Guest"

Daily Reflection

Daily Check-In & Journaling

Capture your feelings accurately using emotion chips, multiline journal prompts, and intelligent real-time sentiment analysis.

🏷️

Curated Emotion Chips

Select from specific emotion tags tailored for impostor feelings: Fraud, Inadequate, Anxious, Worthy, Resilient, Growing.

💡

Smart Sentiment Engine

As you write in the journal text box, relevant emotion chips are dynamically recommended based on sentiment keywords.

✨
Mental State Classification: Based on selected emotions and score weights, Innervy classifies entries into 5 distinct state badges: 🌟 Self-Compassion, ✨ Mindfulness, ☁️ Neutral, 🩹 Self-Criticism, and 🫂 Impostor Syndrome.

Self-Regulation

Mindful Practices & Reframing

Interactive practices built on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) principles to deconstruct imposter thoughts and calm your nervous system.

🔍

The Reality Check

A 5-step guided reframing tool to contrast subjective self-critical thoughts against objective reality.

🌬️

Deep Breath Session

Guided rhythmic breathing exercise with expanding visual animations for acute stress relief.

🫂

Self-Compassion Break

Structured 3-step exercise focusing on mindfulness, common humanity, and self-kindness.

Growth Journey

Reflection Calendar & History

Review your emotional patterns over time with interactive timeline views, color-coded calendar markers, and entry editing tools.

📅

Day / Week / Month Views

Switch between daily timelines, weekly trends, and monthly calendar heatmaps with custom state-color indicators.

Edit & Manage Entries

Tap any past entry to adjust journal notes or emotions. Deletions require a safety confirmation dialog.

Future Date Protection: Innervy includes strict date validation to prevent logging reflections for future dates, ensuring accurate timeline integrity.
