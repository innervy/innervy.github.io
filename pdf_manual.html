01

Onboarding & Authentication

Innervy supports flexible user entry with two distinct sign-in paths to accommodate privacy preferences and offline availability.

🔑 Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0)

Authenticates using Google OAuth protocols. Pulls essential profile data (display name, email address, avatar) to personalize the dashboard experience across app re-launches.

👤 Anonymous Guest Mode

Provides single-tap entry without account registration or remote backend calls. All data is kept strictly inside an isolated guest database.

Privacy Isolation Guarantee: Every signed-in user or Guest account operates inside its own isolated Hive database box. Switching accounts automatically unmounts current isolates and mounts the selected account's data.

04

Daily Check-In & Journaling

Capture complex emotions with curated tags and automated sentiment analysis.

  • Curated Emotion Chips: Select tags specific to impostor feelings (e.g., Fraud, Inadequate, Anxious, Worthy, Resilient, Growing).
  • Smart Sentiment Engine: Evaluates text input in real time to recommend matching emotion chips automatically.
  • Mental State Classification: Entries compute into 5 distinct categories:
    • 🌟 Self-Compassion: High self-kindness and growth orientation.
    • ✨ Mindfulness: Balanced, grounded self-observation.
    • ☁️ Neutral: Objective or steady baseline state.
    • 🩹 Self-Criticism: Mild impostor or critical thoughts.
    • 🫂 Impostor Syndrome: Strong feelings of inadequacy or fraudulence.
05

Mindful Practices & CBT Tools

Interactive exercises designed to deconstruct negative thought loops and soothe nervous system arousal.

🔍 The Reality Check

A 5-step cognitive reframing exercise contrasting subjective self-criticism against objective facts.

🌬️ Deep Breath Session

Guided 4-7-8 rhythmic breathing timer with visual cues for acute stress relief.

🫂 Compassion Break

Guided 3-step practice centering mindfulness, common humanity, and self-kindness affirmations.

06

Reflection Calendar & History

Review emotional patterns across time with multi-view history views.

📅 Timeline & Heatmaps

Switch between Day, Week, and Month views featuring color-coded mental state indicators.

✏️ Management & Protection

Edit past journal entries or delete with safety prompts. Features strict future-date protection to prevent logging invalid entries.

07

Reminders & Background Quotes

Set native system alarms to deliver inspirational quotes at chosen times.

Timezone-Aware Alarms: Reminders use native exact alarm permissions and device timezone offsets. Notifications present inspiring quotes tailored to support self-compassion even when the app is closed.
